## Verdict

Can proceed with caution. Consider addressing warnings.

The three warnings are planning-quality issues that will surface during implementation: FS-1 will break task 1.3 as written unless the implementation adds an explicit `line-height`; CM-1 leaves the spec's tooltip scenario untraceable to a requirement; CM-2 leaves the ellipsis unverified. No blocking errors and no regression candidates (no prior audits with a `## Resolved` header exist).
