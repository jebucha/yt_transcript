### Verdict

**Can proceed with caution.** There are no blocking errors. The two warnings (AC-1, FS-1) describe the same design gap — `executor` is passed to `asyncio.to_thread()` but not accepted by `summarize_with_provider()`. This can be resolved during implementation by adding an `executor=None` parameter to both `summarize_with_provider()` and `_call_chat_completions()` and forwarding it through. The four Info findings are non-blocking (missing Test Strategy section, AGENTS.md convention on test commands, integration-level task without spec mapping).
